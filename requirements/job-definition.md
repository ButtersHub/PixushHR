> Status: Open · Full-time · Negotiable · 1 month · 4 applicants

## Summary

Fully automate the operational execution of employee **onboarding** and **offboarding**
processes at Papaya Global — two of the most sensitive and human-critical touchpoints in
the employee lifecycle.

The agent will be triggered by events in the recruitment system (Comeet / Spark Hire) or
HRIS (Shapes), and will autonomously execute all steps end-to-end with minimal human
intervention.

## Critical Requirements

- Direct, two-way communication with employees in a respectful, warm, and professional tone
- Answer employee questions with empathy, clarity, and timeliness
- Represent the Papaya brand with care throughout all interactions
- Handle all employee data with full confidentiality
- Support Employee Branding by surfacing relevant Papaya content to new joiners

## Systems Integration

Comeet (recruitment), Shapes (HRIS), Microsoft Teams, Trello, and internal systems with
full access.

**Capabilities expected:** email management, calendar management, data analysis, project
management, writing, research, customer service, content creation.

## Task Specification

### Offboarding Workflow

**Trigger:** offboarding event in Shapes HRIS

1. Send pre-offboarding email to departing employee (warm, respectful tone) with all
   last-day information
2. Remain available to answer employee questions throughout process
3. Update all required termination fields in Shapes (date, reason, status)
4. Generate and send calendar invite for last working day to all relevant parties
5. Activate offboarding workflow in Shapes
6. Generate personalized termination letter using HRIS employee data

### Onboarding Workflow

**Trigger:** new hire confirmed in Comeet

1. Extract new employee details from signed contract in Comeet
2. Collect additional info from hiring manager or department peer
3. Trigger onboarding workflow in Shapes and populate required fields
4. Add new hire to relevant Teams lists
5. Send welcome communication in warm and engaging tone
6. Share Papaya Employee Branding content (culture videos, company highlights, Papaya's story)
7. Answer any new employee questions with accurate, friendly, professional responses

## Constraints

- Never share, expose, or transmit employee personal data outside authorized internal systems
- All employee-facing communications must be respectful, warm, and professional — no cold,
  automated, or impersonal language
- Never ignore or dismiss an employee question
- All actions must be logged and auditable
- Full compliance with applicable data protection regulations required
